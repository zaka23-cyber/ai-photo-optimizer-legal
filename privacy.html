2. Your photos on this iPhone

You choose 3–8 photographs using Apple's system photo picker. The app does not request general access to your photo library. Apple's picker may retrieve a selected iCloud photograph.

The app saves working JPEGs, previews, app-generated photo identifiers, dimensions, order and integrity hashes in protected storage on the iPhone. Source Photos identifiers are not saved. Location, capture-date and camera-identifying metadata are discarded. Photo, consent, result and App Attest installation files are protected and excluded from app backups. Your platform choice and onboarding preferences use the app's existing iOS preferences storage.

Selecting or saving photographs does not authorize AI sharing. Refusing analysis leaves local photo management available. The app has no advertising, tracking SDK, analytics SDK, login, payment or subscription implementation in this version.

3. Optional AI photo analysis

Analysis is optional and currently disabled. Once separately approved and enabled, it evaluates photographic quality and suggests a lead photograph and order for your chosen dating platform. The model is instructed not to identify people, infer sensitive traits, rate attractiveness or predict dating success. AI output can still be mistaken.

Before each request, you must review the verified processing details and actively check the OpenAI sharing checkbox. The checkbox starts unchecked. A saved choice never automatically starts a new analysis. Material changes to the processing details require a new choice.

Our service receives resized JPEG copies of the selected photos, their app-generated IDs and dimensions, photo order, current lead choice, dating-platform choice and a request ID. The copies are limited to 1,024 pixels on the longest side and do not replace the saved working photos. The service also receives your consent version, revision, purpose and disclosure digest, plus App Attest information used to verify the app and prevent replay and misuse.

OpenAI receives the selected JPEGs, photo/request IDs, dating platform, order and current lead choice, together with the photographic-analysis instructions. Source Photos identifiers, location/capture metadata, local working-photo integrity hashes and the App Attest and consent-security records are not included in the OpenAI prompt.

4. Providers and processing locations

Apple provides iOS Photos and App Attest services. Supabase hosts our processing function and private database. OpenAI provides the photo analysis. Network providers can process connection information when transmitting requests. There is no sale of photo data or advertising integration in this implementation.

The Supabase database is in Ireland (eu-west-1). The processing function is globally distributed and is not pinned to the database's region. OpenAI requests use its standard global endpoint. Processing exclusively within the EU is not configured or promised. Applicable processor agreements and international-transfer arrangements must be confirmed during the owner's publication review.

5. Storage and retention

Local photos remain until you remove them or clear the app's selection. Saved results contain observations, suggested ordering and selection references, but no second set of photo files. They remain until separately deleted. A changed selection cannot display an old result as a current recommendation. Consent stores the latest versioned decision; deleting a result leaves only a revision marker to prevent a delayed write restoring it.

Photo bytes and analysis text pass through the processing function's memory. Our code does not save them to the database, Supabase Storage or application logs. This is not a promise that providers keep no operational or security records.

The private database retains installation/key identifiers, a public attestation key and replay counter, consent state/version/revision, request IDs and digests, job status, timestamps and spending reservations/charges. The identifiers can link activity to an installation even without a named account. Raw attestation/assertion blobs are verified rather than stored as database fields. Job records do not contain the photos, photo IDs, dating-platform choice or analysis text.

Installation, consent, job and budget records currently have no fixed automatic expiry. They support app verification, abuse prevention, cancellation and cumulative spending enforcement. Challenges expire after two minutes; successful challenge requests opportunistically remove challenges expired for more than one day. There is no scheduled general purge. An inactive service may retain expired challenge rows longer. The operator must review necessity and handle valid deletion requests without resetting spending totals or treating uncertain provider work as free.

Supabase can retain connection and service logs, including IP, browser/client and request-status metadata. Its Free plan lists one-day API/database log retention; this is not a universal 24-hour erasure promise for every provider security record.

OpenAI abuse-monitoring records normally last up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. The app requests store:false and in-memory prompt caching; cache entries can last up to one hour. Neither setting guarantees zero retention.

7. Your controls and deletion requests

In AI Privacy, Withdraw AI consent stops future sharing from this iPhone, invalidates current local authorization and asks the service to stop pending work. Started provider processing may finish. Late results are discarded; a failed server confirmation is shown in the app. Withdrawal does not undo earlier processing or automatically erase records.

Delete local analysis results and Clear local photos are separate controls. They leave originals in Photos unchanged. Local deletion does not delete backend security/spending records or provider logs. Deleting the app is subject to iOS behavior; ordinary file deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure.

For access, correction or deletion of server-side personal data, contact the privacy email above once it is supplied. Do not email API keys or extra photographs. We will seek only the information necessary to locate and verify the relevant records and explain any lawful limits or retention that remains necessary. There is no automatic provider-wide deletion endpoint in the app.

8. Purposes, legal bases and rights

The proposed legal bases for owner review are: providing requested local app functions as necessary to perform the service, consent for optional photo sharing and analysis, and legitimate interests in proportionate security, fraud prevention and spending controls. The latter does not make all technical records exempt from deletion. Any legal-obligation basis must relate to an applicable obligation, rather than being assumed for every log.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. These rights have legal conditions. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully completed beforehand. You may complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or another competent authority. Mandatory privacy rights are preserved.
