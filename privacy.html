2. Your photos on this iPhone

You can choose up to eight photographs using Apple's system photo picker. Photo analysis requires 3–8 saved photos; a single saved photo is sufficient for enhancement. The app does not request general access to your photo library. Apple's picker may retrieve a selected iCloud photograph.

The app saves working JPEGs, previews, app-generated photo identifiers, dimensions, order and integrity hashes in protected storage on the iPhone. Source Photos identifiers are not saved. Location, capture-date and camera-identifying metadata are discarded. Photo, consent, result and App Attest installation files are protected and excluded from app backups. Your platform choice and onboarding preferences use the app's existing iOS preferences storage.

Selecting or saving photographs does not authorize AI sharing. Refusing analysis leaves local photo management available. Local photo management does not require an account. The app uses RevenueCat for purchase verification and purchase analytics. No advertising integration is enabled; purchase analytics are separate from permission to process photos or bio text.

3. Optional AI photo analysis

AI analysis is optional. When you request it, the service evaluates photographic quality and suggests a lead photograph and order for your chosen dating platform. The model is instructed not to identify people, infer sensitive traits, rate attractiveness or predict dating success. AI output can still be mistaken.

Photo analysis, photo enhancement and bio writing each require their own separate permission after you review the processing details. In version 1.0.0 (build 17) and later, each valid permission is remembered in protected storage on this iPhone for the local guest or purchase identity that gave it until you withdraw it or material changes to the provider, purpose or processing details require renewed permission. Switching to a different identity does not give it another identity’s permission; linking the same guest identity with Apple does not itself require permission again. When an older saved permission cannot be linked to its owner, we ask you to allow that purpose again. Signing in or purchasing does not establish who gave an older permission. Allow only records permission and never starts an AI request. Each analysis or generation still requires your explicit Analyze or Generate action. If the local permission files are removed, permission must be given again; iOS Offload can preserve app documents and data and is not a fresh-data installation.

Our service receives resized JPEG copies of the selected photos, their app-generated IDs and dimensions, photo order, current lead choice, dating-platform choice and a request ID. The copies are limited to 1,024 pixels on the longest side and do not replace the saved working photos. The service also receives your consent version, revision, purpose and disclosure digest, plus App Attest information used to verify the app and prevent replay and misuse.

OpenAI receives the selected JPEGs, photo/request IDs, dating platform, order and current lead choice, together with the photographic-analysis instructions. Source Photos identifiers, location/capture metadata, local working-photo integrity hashes and the App Attest and consent-security records are not included in the OpenAI prompt.

Single-photo enhancement

Optional photo enhancement processes one saved photo at a time. You choose Balance lighting, Natural colors or Gentle detail and explicitly give enhancement permission after reviewing the processing disclosure. Your choice is remembered in protected storage on this iPhone until you withdraw it in Enhancement privacy or Improve photo. Changing the photo or improvement, reopening the flow or restarting the app does not reset valid permission. Changes to the provider, purpose or processing disclosure require renewed permission. Analysis permission does not authorize enhancement. Each image is sent only when you tap Generate improved photo.

Our service receives one JPEG copy, its app-generated photo ID and dimensions, the selected improvement, request ID and versioned permission/disclosure digest, plus App Attest verification. The copy is prepared from the saved working image, limited to 1,536 pixels on its longest side and 3 MiB. Source location and capture metadata are removed. OpenAI receives the JPEG and a fixed prompt for the selected photographic correction. The dating platform, other saved photos, analysis results, photo/request IDs, local integrity fingerprint and App Attest records are not included in the enhancement prompt.

The prompt asks OpenAI to preserve the person’s identity, facial geometry, expression, natural skin texture, body, clothing and scene. AI can still alter or invent details. Compare the result with the original and discard anything inaccurate or misleading. Remembered permission never starts a generation automatically; no result is automatically posted, saved to Photos or substituted for the original.

3a. Optional bio writing

When you explicitly generate bio suggestions after granting separate bio-processing permission, your supplied facts, optional existing bio, selected dating platform, tone and request ID pass through our Supabase-hosted service to OpenAI to write three English dating bio suggestions. No photos or photo-analysis results are sent for this purpose. Our service also receives versioned bio permission and App Attest verification. We do not connect to or post to dating accounts.

Bio input and output pass through server memory; our application code does not save this text in the database or Storage. Installation, consent, request and spending metadata follow the separate purposes and deletion criteria in Section 5; completed provider-request identifiers are cleared after 30 days once their charge is resolved, in the next daily cleanup. Your writing draft, source draft, edited suggestions, pending request ID and separate bio permission are saved in protected storage on this iPhone and excluded from app backups. Copy writes your selected text to the local clipboard only when requested; copies outside the app are separate.

The existing operator, contact details, Supabase hosting and international-processing disclosures also apply to bio writing. The database is in Ireland; function and OpenAI processing are not limited to the EU. OpenAI abuse-monitoring logs normally last up to 30 days, subject to legal and safety exceptions. Our requests explicitly select in-memory prompt caching; OpenAI documents entries lasting up to one hour for that mode. This is separate from provider abuse-monitoring retention. Optional OpenAI training sharing is disabled. store:false does not promise zero retention.

Bio permission is remembered until withdrawn in Bio privacy and must be renewed after material changes to provider, purpose or processing details. Granting permission does not generate text: each request requires an explicit Generate action. Photo-analysis and enhancement permissions remain separate. Withdrawal stops future bio submissions from this iPhone and requests cancellation of pending bio work. Already-started processing may finish; cancellation cannot recall data already sent. Late results are discarded. Delete local bio writing separately in Bio privacy. Local deletion and withdrawal do not erase server security/spending records, provider logs or text copied elsewhere.

3b. Purchase identity, optional Apple sign-in, subscriptions and image credits

When you first choose a purchase or Restore Purchases, Supabase Auth creates a protected, pseudonymous purchase identity without asking you for a name, email address, password or Apple sign-in. The app reuses its saved session in a non-synchronizing, device-only Keychain item. Supabase processes this identifier, authentication sessions and related security metadata. This is pseudonymous processing, not a promise that your activity is anonymous.

Sign in with Apple is optional in Settings. You may link it to your existing purchase identity without starting a purchase or receiving free credits. Apple supplies an app-specific identity and may supply an email address, including a private relay address; we do not request your name. Linking preserves the existing purchase identity and its remaining server balance. If that Apple identity is already linked to a different purchase account, a guest identity with purchases or a remaining balance is preserved rather than silently replaced or merged. After a fresh server check confirms that the guest identity is empty, optional sign-in may instead recover the existing Apple-linked account without merging balances. Use the same successfully linked Apple identity to recover its account on another device. Your Apple sign-in account and your App Store payment account may be different.

Restore Purchases checks App Store subscription rights using the relevant App Store payment account. A verified Restore may reassign an existing paid-period allocation to the current purchase identity while preserving its quantity, use and original expiry; it does not create a new paid week or refill spent or deleted allowances. Verification of a completed Apple purchase, including a retry after a lost response, may reconcile the same transferred receipt without refilling older allocations or starting another payment. Finished consumable image-credit purchases are not automatically recoverable from Apple Restore alone. We can recover an existing remaining consumable allocation during Restore only when RevenueCat verifies the same transaction and that allocation still exists in our server ledger. Missing or previously deleted consumable credits are not reconstructed. If this device's saved guest session is no longer available and you did not successfully link Apple sign-in beforehand, recovery of the remaining consumable balance is not guaranteed. Optional linking provides recovery through the same account's server history; a support address alone is not a recovery method.

We use RevenueCat to verify App Store subscriptions and consumable image-credit purchases. Our server assigns a stable, app-specific customer identifier linked to your Supabase account; this is pseudonymous, not anonymous. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription history, product and transaction identifiers, purchase and expiration times, subscription status, and app/device context such as app and OS version, locale, currency and connection information. We use its purchase dashboard and analytics. The app does not send your photos, bio text, name or email address to RevenueCat as customer attributes. Automatic device-identifier collection and optional RevenueCat diagnostics are disabled, and no external advertising or analytics integration is enabled for this project. These settings do not stop the purchase processing described here.

To verify the app's purchase environment and build and check purchase availability, our service also processes Apple's signed app transaction, which can include an app-transaction identifier and device-verification metadata. Our application code verifies this proof in request memory, does not store the raw proof in the purchase ledger or application logs, and does not send it to OpenAI. Environment-specific purchase verification timestamps are linked to the purchase account and removed when account deletion completes. Candidate-level operator spending and attempt totals contain no account or installation identifier; they remain while the service's operator spending controls are operated and are not a replacement for a customer's credit balance.

Apple handles payment details. Our app and backend do not receive your payment-card number. Our private Supabase database records verified purchase periods, credit grants, quantities, expiry, reservations, delivered or returned credits, transaction sources and verification timestamps. Weekly allowances and purchased image credits are separate. Purchased credits do not expire and do not require an active subscription; weekly image credits are used first. Failed verification is shown as unavailable or as the last verified balance, rather than a new zero balance.

Purchasing, signing in, restoring purchases or managing a subscription does not grant AI-processing permission and never starts generation automatically. The separate photo-analysis, enhancement and bio permissions described above continue to apply. Sandbox and production purchase records are kept separate. The service accepts only an approved, cryptographically verified App Store or TestFlight candidate. Apple Review uses verified sandbox purchases with the same product quotas, separate AI permissions and safety controls; sandbox rights never grant production allowances. There is no special free review access or automatic generation after purchase.

One unit is reserved atomically with each admitted AI job. A validated usable result is acknowledged by this iPhone; repeated acknowledgements do not consume another unit. Failed or cancelled work without a usable delivered result returns the original allocation without extending its expiry. Unresolved work blocks another debit and never restarts automatically. Pending delivery acknowledgements contain only job identifiers, are separated by purchase account in protected local storage excluded from backups, and are removed after acknowledgement or confirmed deletion of that purchase account. They contain no photo or bio content. Operator API costs are recorded separately and may remain payable when your unit is returned.

5. Storage and retention

Local photos remain until you remove them or clear the app's selection. Saved results contain observations, suggested ordering and selection references, but no second set of photo files. They remain until separately deleted. A changed selection cannot display an old result as a current recommendation. Consent stores the latest versioned decision; deleting a result leaves only a revision marker to prevent a delayed write restoring it.

Photo bytes, analysis text and generated image bytes pass through the processing function's memory. Our code does not save them to the database, Supabase Storage or application logs. This is not a promise that providers keep no operational or security records.

The private database retains installation/key identifiers, a public attestation key and replay counter, consent state/version/revision, request IDs and digests, job status, timestamps and spending reservations/charges. The identifiers can link activity to an installation even without a named account. Raw attestation/assertion blobs are verified rather than stored as database fields. Job records do not contain the photos, photo IDs, dating-platform choice or analysis text.

A received, validated enhancement result that is successfully retained is kept in protected on-device app storage for its source photo and the existing guest or Apple-linked purchase identity. These files are excluded from app backups. Under that same identity, reopening Improve photo can show the retained comparison after an app restart without a new AI request or another credit. It remains until you explicitly discard it, successfully save it to Photos, remove its source photo or complete purchase-account deletion. When Photos saving succeeds but local removal fails, the app reports that the recovery copy remains; removing the source photo from the app also removes that copy. Signing out hides the previous identity’s comparison rather than transferring it to another account. Storage or file-read failures can prevent recovery; the app reports these failures rather than claiming that the result has been safely retained. Ordinary backgrounding does not discard a retained comparison. This recovery does not cover a result that was not received, validated and retained before cancellation or process termination, and it is not a promise of permanent storage. The Save to Photos action requests only Add Photos permission and creates a separate AI-improved JPEG copy. The saved original, app selection and lead photo stay unchanged. Once you start a Photos save, app cancellation cannot undo a committed Photos write. Copies you saved to Photos must be managed or deleted in Photos; clearing app photos or analysis results does not delete those copies.

Enhancement job records additionally retain the requested image-model snapshot, text/image input and image output token counts where available, and the provider request ID where available. These records do not contain the photo or generated image. Closing the app or deleting local files does not erase provider logs or server security/spending records.

Installation keys, replay counters, current AI-consent state and job/spending records support app verification, idempotent requests, cancellation and spending limits while an installation key remains active. For a billable AI job, a verified purchase account is linked to the App Attest installation through that job’s credit reservation. This account-to-job link is removed on completed account deletion. The underlying installation security and operator-cost records follow the separate criteria below. For a separately verified installation-erasure request, the operator retires the key, reconciles any active or uncertain job and retires any operator authorization tied to it, then removes that installation and its consent, job, challenge and individual-budget records. Global spending totals remain without an installation identifier. We do not claim that these records must be retained under a particular law. Completed enhancement and bio provider-request identifiers are cleared after 30 days in the next daily cleanup once a charge is resolved. Challenges expire after two minutes and are removed in the next daily cleanup, or earlier by existing opportunistic cleanup. Service outages or a paused project can delay scheduled work until service resumes.

Supabase can retain connection and service logs, including IP, browser/client and request-status metadata. Its Free plan lists one-day API/database log retention; this is not a universal 24-hour erasure promise for every provider security record.

OpenAI abuse-monitoring records normally last up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Analysis and bio requests explicitly use store:false and prompt_cache_retention:in_memory. OpenAI documents cache entries lasting up to one hour for that mode. Its general data-controls documentation also describes encrypted prompt-cache application state lasting up to 24 hours for supported models and organization settings. These cache descriptions are separate from abuse-monitoring retention; we do not promise that all provider copies disappear when an in-memory cache entry expires. Enhancement uses the direct Images edit API without a stored Responses conversation or Files API upload; standard OpenAI provider retention still applies. Neither setting guarantees zero retention.

While you keep a purchase identity, Supabase Auth holds its pseudonymous identifier, authentication sessions and security metadata and, only if you choose Apple linking, the linked Apple identity and any supplied email. The private purchase ledger holds the customer mapping and credit history. These support authenticated purchase ownership, nonexpiring purchased credits and Restore without resetting spent credits. Expired weekly-grant history with no job reference is removed 30 days after its period ends in the next daily cleanup; referenced grants remain only with the corresponding account/job history until deletion. After a verified Restore or completed Apple purchase changes an allocation’s owner, completed job records belonging to the previous identity follow that identity’s existing retention criteria. If the moved allocation is later deleted, only its obsolete allocation reference is removed from those completed records; another identity’s job history is not erased by this deletion. On completed deletion of the guest or Apple-linked purchase identity, the Auth user and sessions, RevenueCat customer, Supabase-to-RevenueCat mapping, credit grants and reservations are removed. The app confirms RevenueCat's queued deletion by checking that the customer is no longer available before reporting completion.

Two minimal records remain after purchase-account deletion. First, a keyed fingerprint of the verified Apple store transaction/product identity (and paid period for a subscription) prevents the same historical purchase from granting credits again. It contains no Apple subject, email, Supabase UUID, RevenueCat customer ID, credit quantity or spent balance. It is pseudonymous rather than claimed anonymous. A subscription-period fingerprint is removed after that period ends in the next daily cleanup. A nonexpiring consumable fingerprint remains only while this app/environment's purchase reconciliation can accept that historical transaction; it is deleted when that purchase-processing catalog is permanently retired. This is an operational replay-protection criterion, not an invented legal accounting obligation.

Second, an unlinked receipt hash, completion time and manual-Apple-revocation flag remain for 30 days after completion and are removed in the next daily cleanup. They allow confirmation after a lost final response and cannot restore an account or credits. While deletion is unfinished, the required account/customer identifiers and phase remain solely to finish the confirmed request. They are removed at completion. An Apple refresh token used for deletion is encrypted during retry, removed after acknowledged revocation, and no longer usable from our retry record after seven days; its stored ciphertext is cleared in the next daily cleanup. A fresh authenticated Apple confirmation is then required. Pending records remain until the interrupted deletion is completed; retrying does not create a new purchase.

If authentication events are written to the optional Auth audit table, records referencing the deleted user UUID are removed at completion; remaining audit entries are removed after 30 days in the next daily cleanup. That table contained no records when inspected. Platform connection/service logs and provider backup or security records are controlled by the providers, not erased by our database cleanup. RevenueCat's customer-deletion API is used for its customer record; its published DPA reserves standard backup/archival practices without a fixed per-user backup-erasure deadline. Supabase's published Free-plan log-access period is not a universal erasure guarantee. Exact residual backup/security-log deletion deadlines for this project are not verified; no blanket retention or EU-only claim is made.

The daily cleanup runs in the existing Supabase database. Its 7- and 30-day periods are operational minimization choices, not claims of statutory retention requirements. A daily batch can add up to one day during normal operation; outages or project suspension delay execution until recovery. The separately documented OpenAI retention and local-photo/bio controls continue to apply.

7. Your controls and deletion requests

For analysis, in AI Privacy, Withdraw AI consent stops future analysis sharing from this iPhone, invalidates current local authorization and asks the service to stop pending work. Started provider processing may finish. Late results are discarded; a failed server confirmation is shown in the app. Withdrawal does not undo earlier processing or automatically erase records.

For enhancement, Withdraw enhancement permission turns off future sharing on this iPhone. During an enhancement it also stops local waiting, discards late results and asks the service to cancel pending work. Cancel or close stops waiting for a pending request without withdrawing remembered permission. Backgrounding also stops waiting for pending work; it does not discard an already received comparison that has been retained locally as described in section 5. Processing already started may finish; cancellation cannot recall data already sent to the provider. Each new generation requires your explicit Generate tap; there is no automatic retry.

Delete local analysis results and Clear local photos are separate controls. They leave originals in Photos unchanged. Local deletion does not delete backend security/spending records or provider logs. Deleting the app is subject to iOS behavior; ordinary file deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure.

For access, correction or deletion of server-side personal data, contact the privacy email above. Do not email API keys or extra photographs. We will seek only the information necessary to locate and verify the relevant records and explain any lawful limits or retention that remains necessary. There is no automatic provider-wide deletion endpoint in the app.

Guest or Apple-linked purchase-account deletion. In Settings → Subscription & credits → Delete purchase account, review the consequences before confirming. You can keep the account and its nonexpiring purchased-credit balance, postpone deletion to use credits when AI service is available, or delete immediately. Immediate deletion permanently removes the remaining account balance; it cannot be restored by a new sign-in, Apple Restore or support contact. You can separately request a refund from Apple before or after deleting; Apple decides eligibility and no refund is guaranteed. Deletion neither cancels an Apple subscription nor stops Apple's billing. Manage or cancel an active subscription separately through Apple's supported interface. Mandatory rights are not waived. If server deletion is interrupted, use Check deletion status. After server confirmation, interrupted local cleanup can be resumed with Finish local cleanup. Queued or failed service responses are not reported as completed.

After the server confirms purchase-account deletion, the app removes local photo copies, bio drafts and suggestions, saved analysis and generated results, temporary files and account-related caches. It clears the platform choice and remembered AI permissions and returns to the existing introduction. Images in Apple Photos, including originals and previously exported copies, are not deleted. The existing secure deletion receipt retains a local server-confirmed cleanup marker until local cleanup completes, so an interruption can be resumed without recreating an account or its balance. While server deletion is unconfirmed, this final local erasure does not run. An ordinary sign-out, restart or app update does not clear local content or restart onboarding. App Attest and installation-security identifiers are preserved to maintain the existing abuse and replay controls. Signing out is not account deletion. For an Apple-linked purchase account, if manual Apple revocation is required, open iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Sign in with Apple, select this app and stop using Sign in with Apple for the app. An unlinked guest purchase identity has no app Apple-sign-in authorization to revoke.

8. Purposes, legal bases and rights

We process account and purchase data to authenticate you, verify transactions and administer the purchase balance you request. We use purchase analytics to understand subscription and purchase activity. We process data to provide the local app functions you request, rely on consent for optional photo sharing, analysis, enhancement and bio writing, and rely on legitimate interests in proportionate security, fraud prevention and spending controls. These interests do not make all technical records exempt from deletion. Where a legal obligation requires retention, it applies only to the relevant records.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. These rights have legal conditions. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully completed beforehand. You may complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or another competent authority. Mandatory privacy rights are preserved.
